# Nexus Financial Services: GRC Framework Review

A Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) case study completed as part of the **GRC Framework Integration** class with [10Alytics](https://www.linkedin.com/company/10alytics/).

---

## Scenario

Nexus Financial Services is a fast-growing financial company in Lagos, Nigeria, with **$800M in assets** and **no formal GRC framework**. Decisions are made informally, and no one owns policy, risk, or regulatory tracking.

**My role:** Junior GRC Analyst. I was asked to review what was going wrong, categorise each issue under the correct GRC pillar, and recommend one action to improve.

---

## What I Did

1. Explained Governance, Risk Management, and Compliance in plain language
2. Identified three issues from the case study
3. Mapped each issue to a GRC pillar
4. Recommended one action to improve Nexus's GRC programme

---

## Findings

| # | Issue | GRC Pillar |
|---|-------|-----------|
| 1 | No clear process for approving or enforcing company policies | **Governance** |
| 2 | No standard way of assessing risk before making business decisions | **Risk Management** |
| 3 | No one responsible for tracking regulatory requirements and deadlines | **Compliance** |

**Key insight:** The three issues are connected. Weak governance is the root cause, because without clear ownership, nobody is accountable for risk or compliance either.

---

## Recommendation

**Form a GRC Steering Committee and give every key task a named owner.**

- Bring together senior leaders and representatives from operations, finance, legal, and IT
- Name owners for policy approval, risk assessment (Risk Manager role), and regulatory tracking (Compliance Analyst role)
- Set a simple policy life cycle: write, review, approve, communicate, enforce, review annually

**Why governance first?** It is the foundation. Once ownership and authority exist, a risk checklist and a compliance calendar can be added and actually used.

---

## Key Takeaways

- GRC is a **process, not a product**
- Growth without structure is a risk in itself
- Good governance makes risk management and compliance work
